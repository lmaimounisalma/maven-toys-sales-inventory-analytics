# Maven Toys — Sales & Inventory Analytics

## Project Overview

Maven Toys — Sales & Inventory Analytics is a business intelligence project built with Microsoft Excel using a large retail sales dataset.

The project analyzes sales performance, product categories, store performance, geographic distribution, inventory levels, and year-over-year trends to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

The final deliverable includes a structured analytical workbook, an executive dashboard, and a professional business report.

---

## Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze overall sales and transaction performance
- Identify the best-performing products and product categories
- Compare store performance
- Analyze sales across different cities
- Monitor inventory levels
- Identify year-over-year performance changes
- Build executive-level KPIs and visualizations
- Provide actionable recommendations based on the data

## Sales Performance

The analysis covers sales from January 2022 through September 2023.

Because 2023 contains only January–September data, year-over-year comparisons use the same January–September period to ensure a fair comparison.

### Year-over-Year Growth

- Sales increased by **30.9%**
- Units sold increased by **40.8%**
- Transactions increased by **37.5%**

These results indicate strong growth in both sales volume and customer purchasing activity.

## Product Category Performance

**Toys** is the strongest category, generating more than one-third of total revenue.

## Key Business Insights

1. **Strong year-over-year growth:** Sales increased by 30.9% when comparing January–September 2023 with the same period in 2022.

2. **Toys dominate revenue:** The Toys category generated 35.26% of total revenue.

3. **Category concentration:** Toys and Games together represent 50.7% of total sales.

4. **Strong product concentration:** A small number of products generate a significant share of revenue, with Product 18 being the top performer.

5. **Geographic diversity:** While Ciudad de Mexico is the largest individual market, sales are distributed across many cities.

6. **Inventory visibility:** The analysis provides a foundation for connecting inventory levels with sales demand and future replenishment decisions.

---

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could be considered:

- Prioritize high-performing products and categories such as Toys.
- Monitor inventory levels for products with strong sales demand.
- Investigate the performance drivers of top-performing stores.
- Use geographic sales patterns to support store-level planning.
- Continue monitoring year-over-year growth to identify changing demand patterns.
- Extend the analysis in future versions with inventory turnover, stock coverage, and profitability metrics.
